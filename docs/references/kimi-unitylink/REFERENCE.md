## Role in UnityLink OS

Use this page as the reference for the public-facing platform and acquisition layer, not as the operating-system shell.

## Preserve

- Black background with restrained cyan-to-green glass accents.
- Space Grotesk for major display text and Inter for interface/body text.
- Clear pathways into subscriptions, tracking, jobs, dashboard, community, and onboarding.
- Vehicle-led hero storytelling and direct primary actions.
- Subscription comparison, mobile-app promotion, employment entry, and community participation.
- Typed headline and light entrance motion as optional marketing-page devices.

## Do Not Copy Into the OS Shell

- Large marketing hero inside authenticated workspaces.
- Repeated card-heavy sections where lists, tables, maps, or canvases are more appropriate.
- Static pricing or vehicle claims as authoritative production data.
- Separate simulated assistant behavior.

## Integration Decision

The public platform, Fleet consumer flow, and Hermes-powered operating workspace share design tokens, account identity, command navigation, and context continuity. Their information density and page anatomy remain purpose-specific.
